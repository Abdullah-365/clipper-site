Clipper

Clipper is a personal, non-commercial tool that turns highlights from livestreams into short vertical videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel (and other social accounts the operator owns).

It only clips streams from creators who run clipping campaigns or have given permission, and every clip credits the original creator.

Clipper uses the YouTube API Services solely to upload videos to the operator's own channel. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
